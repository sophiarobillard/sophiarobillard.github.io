About Me!

My name is Sophia Robillard

I am currently an undergraduate at the University of Washington majoring in Earth & Space Sciences with a minor in Data Science! I am passionate about sedimentary systems and how we can use them to learn about Earth's history!

As an undergraduate researcher in the Δ*Isolab, I use clumped-isotope geochemistry and carbonate petrography to investigate the origins of fine-grained lacustrine carbonate rocks from the Colorado Plateau. More specifically, I look at micro-scale biological processes, microbially mediated carbonate formation conditions, and isotopic disequilibrium to see how they alter geochemical data for paleoenvironment reconstructions.
